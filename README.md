Conversational vehicle troubleshooting assistant developed for the **GenAI “Coding with Generative AI” Hackathon**, earning **2nd place**.

### ⚖️ [Tunisian Law AI Assistant](https://github.com/khalil-dev916/Tunisian-Law-AI-Assistant)

Multilingual legal-information assistant developed for the **second edition of the GenAI “Coding with Generative AI” Hackathon**, earning **4th place**.

- Answers questions about Tunisian law using **Groq API** and a local legal knowledge base
- Supports **Arabic, French, and English**, including right-to-left Arabic layouts
- Processes legal documents with **Mammoth** and splits content into searchable chunks
- Selects relevant legal passages using keyword-based relevance scoring
- Combines a **Next.js and TypeScript** chat application with **Tailwind CSS** styling
- Provides general legal information and orientation
